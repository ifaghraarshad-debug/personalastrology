ASTROLOGY FAQ

Real chart. Real context.

Everything starts with your birth data and is designed for reflection and self-discovery.

What is a birth chart?

A birth chart is a snapshot of the sky at the time and place you were born. It maps the Sun, Moon, planets and key chart points across the zodiac.

Is Auravie just a daily horoscope?

No. Auravie starts with your natal chart, then connects it with the areas of life you want to explore.

Why do I need my exact birth time?

Your birth time helps calculate your Rising sign and houses more accurately. If you don't know it, you can still explore your chart.

Can astrology predict my future?

Astrology is best used as a reflective framework rather than a guarantee of future events. Auravie focuses on patterns, themes and timing.
